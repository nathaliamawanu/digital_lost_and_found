How FindIt Works

Recovering lost belongings has never been easier.

📝

1. Report an Item

Report a lost or found item by providing its name, description, location and photograph.

🔍

2. Search for Items

Browse reported items or use the search feature to find belongings that match yours.

🤝

3. Connect and Recover

Contact the person who reported the item, verify ownership and arrange its safe recovery.
